### Fixed

- **Culverhouse (opt-in interval-quality policy):**
  - A row merges only into the immediately preceding row of its app sequence, and only when both share the same duration-rule state: minimum/maximum eligibility, blanks, micro-use class and zero length. Excluded time is never bridged into an eligible survivor.
  - Filtered ("bad-app") rows blanked by reconstruction get their interval back before the 10-minute cap, minus any maximum-duration trim. They are capped and credited, as the contract says.
- **Screen-gated crediting (opt-in):**
  - `device_liveness_only` always credits alive spans.
  - Screen capability accepts the fused and device-level labels (`Screen Interactive/Keyguard Shown`, `Screen Non-Interactive/Keyguard Hidden`, `Device Screen Off`).
  - The auto-lock bridge measures to the screen's real return.
  - A blank participant ID finds its own witnesses (the substrate keys blanks as `unknown`).
- **Morrison (opt-in):** an event at or past the 30 s lock timeout closes the episode at the lock.
- **Raw headers:**
  - The decoder and the mixed-study splitter trim headers and strip a byte-order mark, as inspection does.
  - On duplicate headers, inspection now reads the **last** column, which is what execution (a `HashMap`) reads.
- **No-activity placeholder rows:** the day is kept in period and review summaries, but the row is never a session or a top app.
- **Review top apps:** maximum-duration eligibility is honoured. The incremental review overlay now copies every B06 field (stop timestamp, qualified, eligible, drop-pending, trimmed, endpoint reason).
- **XLSX support files:** date-time cells keep their milliseconds.
- **Web:**
  - A workspace head committed by an earlier runtime build is reprocessed instead of rejected.
  - App-only outputs are not requested on screen-only runs.
  - Results carry the options and support files they were produced with. A restored run goes stale once any support file is supplied.
  - Each A/B comparison result shows its own options.
  - Races closed: stale inspection, boot restore and timezone discovery.
  - Retry saves keep the attempted and unfinished counts.
  - ZIP entry names are flagged UTF-8.

The opt-in strategies above change output where the old behavior was the bug. The tests that
pinned the old behavior now assert the corrected values. Default-option goldens did not move.
