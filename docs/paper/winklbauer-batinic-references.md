# Winklbauer & Batinic (2026) — complete reference list

Transcribed verbatim from pp. 35–41 of the SSRN preprint PDF (doi:10.2139/ssrn.7008242),
*From Logs to Metrics: The Impact of Researcher Degrees of Freedom on Smartphone Usage Metrics*.

51 entries. This is the authoritative record of what that paper cited. It is the seed set for the
citation-chase described in `literature-dossier.md` §8.

**Why this list matters to our paper.** Winklbauer & Batinic ran the field's largest multiverse
(16,128 specifications) but started from **already-reconstructed episodes** (their p.32). Their
reference list is therefore a near-complete census of the studies that the field's leading
methodologists consider the relevant prior work on smartphone usage measurement. If those studies
also fail to declare how raw events became episodes, that is not an anecdote — it is the state of
the field, and it is the empirical warrant for the paper's central claim.

**Extraction target for each entry** (see `citation-chase.md` for results):
1. Instrument and its ladder rung (§7.1 of the dossier): screen-on/off, app-level, raw event log,
   or vendor aggregate.
2. Whether a **session threshold** is stated, and its value.
3. Whether the **episode reconstruction rule** (how raw events became episodes) is stated at all.
4. System-app / launcher filtering, if declared.
5. Measurement window, exclusion rules, aggregation choice.
6. Whether the processing code or the processed data is available.

## Notes on the transcription

- Entry 46/47: the Publizistik and CHBR records render the surname unaccented ("Toth"). OpenAlex
  carries **Tóth**. Use the accented form in our prose; keep the unaccented form when quoting a
  publisher record verbatim.
- Entry 24 (Keusch et al. 2020) is deposited without volume/issue in the preprint's list; the
  Sociological Methods & Research record should be checked for the final pagination.
- Entry 2 (Baumeister et al. 2007) and entries 34, 37, 44 (books/chapters) have no DOI in the
  preprint's list except where shown.
